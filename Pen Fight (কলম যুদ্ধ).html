Pen Fight

The back-bench classic. Flick your pen, knock theirs off the desk, keep yours on it.

2 or 3 players, taking turns on one screen.

Pick your pens

Players at the desk

How to play

Every player gets one pen on the desk. Take turns flicking yours into the others. A pen that goes over the edge is out, and the last pen on the desk wins.

On your turn

  1. Pick the side you’ll strike: top, middle or bottom. Use the buttons or tap that part of your pen.
  2. Set the power with the slider.
  3. Drag on the desk to aim. The dotted line shows where your pen will slide.
  4. Press Flick! and watch everything settle before the next turn.

The three sides

Side 1, top
Strikes the top (nib) end, so your pen spins as it slides and sweeps a wide arc. Good for clipping a pen that’s off your straight line.
Side 2, middle
No spin, straight line, shortest reach. On a clean hit your pen stops dead and the target takes nearly all the speed. The best knock-out shot.
Side 3, bottom
Strikes the bottom (cap) end. Your pen turns heavy for the shot and has the longest reach. It bulldozes through, but keeps rolling after the hit, so it can follow the target off the edge.

Power and control

More power means a harder hit and a longer slide, and the guide line gets shorter the harder you flick, so big shots are partly blind.

Rules

  • If any part of your pen crosses the desk edge, you’re out on the spot (you can switch this to “pen’s center” in Settings).
  • Knocking yourself off counts too.
  • If every remaining pen falls in the same turn, it’s a draw.

Keyboard: ← → aim, ↑ ↓ power, 1 2 3 sides, Space to flick.
